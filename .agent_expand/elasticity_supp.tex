\section{Frontier Elasticities}

Write $L=L_m+L_T+L_N$, where
$L_m=C_m m^{-\delta}$, $L_T=C_T(\eta T)^{-\gamma}$, and
$L_N=C_NN^{-\beta}$. Then
\[
-\partial_{\log m}\log L=\delta\frac{L_m}{L},\qquad
-\partial_{\log T}\log L=\gamma\frac{L_T}{L},
\]
with the analogous identity for $N$. Dividing by the exponents proves that the
normalized elasticities equal the three risk shares and sum to one. Centered
finite differences on a logarithmic grid estimate them whenever the additive
proxy is locally accurate.
