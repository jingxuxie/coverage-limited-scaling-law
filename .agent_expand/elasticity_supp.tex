\section{Frontier Elasticities}

For
\[
L=C_m m^{-\delta}+C_T(\eta T)^{-\gamma}+C_NN^{-\beta},
\]
direct differentiation gives
\[
-\partial_{\log m}\log L
=\delta\frac{C_m m^{-\delta}}{L},\qquad
-\partial_{\log T}\log L
=\gamma\frac{C_T(\eta T)^{-\gamma}}{L},
\]
and the analogous identity for $N$.  Dividing by the respective exponents
proves Eq.~\eqref{eq:elasticity-simplex}.  Centered finite differences on a
logarithmic grid estimate these weights whenever the additive scaling proxy is
locally accurate.  The same calculation extends to any finite collection of
additive bottleneck terms: their normalized elasticities form a simplex and are
exactly their fractions of predicted risk.
